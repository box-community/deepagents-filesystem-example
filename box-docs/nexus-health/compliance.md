# Compliance & Security

## HIPAA Compliance
All Nexus Health products are fully HIPAA compliant:
- Business Associate Agreements (BAA) available
- End-to-end encryption (AES-256)
- Audit logging for all PHI access
- Role-based access control

## Data Residency
- US data centers (AWS GovCloud available)
- EU data centers available for GDPR compliance
- No data leaves specified region

## Backup & Recovery
- Daily automated backups
- 30-day retention
- 4-hour RTO, 1-hour RPO
- Disaster recovery included in Enterprise plans

## Certifications
- SOC 2 Type II (annual audit)
- HITRUST CSF r2
- ISO 27001 (in progress)
